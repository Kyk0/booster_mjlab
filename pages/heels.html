K1 in 50 mm heels

3D-printed heel shoes for the Booster K1, and walking policies warm-started in them. Simulation only so far.

How

  • The shoe mesh replaces the foot collision. The shoe mass is merged into the foot inertia, and the sole sites move to the shoe underside.
  • Reference motions for AMP and resets: +15.6° ankle pitch (the shoe sole pitch) and +58.5 mm root height, so the shoe sole is flat where the bare sole was.
  • Warm start from the bare-foot checkpoint. Actor and critic weights are kept; the optimizer and curricula start fresh.

Results (sim)

  • Loco-teleop with full arm motion: falls went from 28% to 3.9% after warm start, with a plateau after about 300 iterations. Still a bit less stable than bare feet (0–2%).
  • Full-body walk in booster_deploy MuJoCo: the bare-foot policy drifts 1.4 m while standing. The warm-started one stands still and tracks 0.5 and 1.0 m/s commands at 0.50 and 0.95 m/s.
