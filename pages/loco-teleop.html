K1 loco-teleop

A walking policy for the legs only. The head and arms are left free for teleop. Simulation only so far.

What changed vs. the full-body walking task

  • The policy outputs 12 leg joints instead of 22. It still observes all joint positions and velocities, so it sees what the arms do.
  • The arms are part of the environment. A generator moves them every step: circles, sweeps and random jumps over the whole joint range, 0.2–1.5 Hz, peaks around 15 rad/s.
  • The arm posture reward is removed. The pose and joint-limit rewards apply to the legs only.
  • Same AMP setup: a legs-only discriminator on LAFAN walking, PPO with Muon, 4096 envs.

Deploy

A booster_deploy task: arm targets over UDP from a teleop script, a 1.5 s ramp-in, rate limits, and heading hold. Tested in MuJoCo sim2sim only.
